Driving the dashboard

  
Zoom

  
Drag a rectangle on the chart to zoom into any region - dense clusters become readable. A thin horizontal or vertical band zooms just the axis it spans. Double-click the chart, press Esc, or hit "reset zoom" to zoom back out. Zoom holds while data refreshes; changing an axis resets it. Double-clicking the detail panel closes it.

  
Filters

  
    
    
  • src / dst contains + src / dst not - smart IP matching: single
    IPs (192.168.0.1), ranges (192.168.0.100-200), shorthand lists
    (192.168.0.1,2,3,6-10,12), CIDR (10.0.0.0/8), or plain substring.
    • 
    
  • ports are / ports not - dest-port lists, e.g. 443, 53, 1000-2000. "ports not" shows everything except those ports.
    • 
    
  • direction - tick any combination: e.g. untick out and other for internal and inbound only.
    • 
    
  • from / to - time window; min count - busiest tallies only.
    • 
  

  
Encodings

  
X / Y position, color and size can each be set to any aspect: time, source, destination, port, protocol, severity, direction, counts. Size also offers per-group aggregates - e.g. "distinct ports per group" sizes each dot by how many different services its device talks to (grouping follows the color variable, then the categorical axis). Records and flows per group act as traffic-quantity proxies - Omada's remote log doesn't include byte or packet totals.

  
Spotlight related traffic

  
Click any dot to spotlight its related traffic: the exact same conversation is highlighted with a white ring, the same host pair stays bright, and traffic to the same service stays visible at reduced intensity - everything else greys out. The stats panel shows metrics for the spotlighted set.

  
Key drilldown

  
Click a color-key entry to filter the entire view to that value (e.g. show only one source IP, or one severity). The entry gets a blue border; click it again to remove the filter. The stats panel reflects the narrowed set, plus any active zoom window.

  
Points

  
Hover a dot for a summary; click it for the full tally entry. Each dot is one known occurrence (tallies) or one raw record.

  
Refresh

  
Data refetches every 30 s (toggle "auto 30s"). Colors and zoom stay stable; the time window slides forward. "refresh" refits the axes; "reset all" restores defaults.
